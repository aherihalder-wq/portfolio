# Aheri Halder · Portfolio

Live site: https://aherihalder-wq.github.io/portfolio/

## How to update
- **Text, projects, resume, links, tools:** edit `content.js` (instructions are at the top of the file).
- **Pictures:** add them to `assets/img/` and write their path in `content.js`.
- Commit the change and the live site updates in a minute or two.

## Files
- `index.html`: main page (hero, What I do, About me, worlds, toolkit, hire me)
- `world.html`: Product Design, UI/UX Design and Phygital Lab pages with the case studies
- `illustration.html`: the Digital Illustration world
- `content.js`: all your content
- `cursor.js`: the yellow pebble cursor
- `icons.js`: app icons for the toolkit
